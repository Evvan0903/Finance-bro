## Conventions adopted in this project

- Lead with concise, falsifiable conclusions and the risks to them.
- Separate actuals, guidance, user inputs, and forecasts in both labels and data structures.
- Put sources, dates, units, and methodology notes next to charts and tables.
- Make assumptions, valuation bridges, limitations, report version, and freshness visible.
- Keep styling original and neutral; use no institution names, logos, proprietary terminology, copied visual identity, or third-party forecasts.
